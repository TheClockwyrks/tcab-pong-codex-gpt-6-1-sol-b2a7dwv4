# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. A court that never quite sits still. Carom is a quick browser duel where sharp returns and unexpected angles decide who owns the next rally. Take on the machine in Solo, or share a keyboard for a local rivalry.

The opening serve gives you a moment to line up your paddle. Catch the ball at the center for a direct return, or near an edge to send it climbing toward the walls. Every paddle hit makes the rally faster, so an easy exchange can become a test of nerve.

Move as you strike and the ball begins to curve. Spin lets a return drift away from its first line, slip around a defender, or find the face of a gold bar. Those bars sway and rotate, giving familiar bank shots a fresh angle each time.

Play to eleven with a two-point lead. At deuce, every return matters: hold your position for a clean shot, or commit to a moving strike and trust the curve. The best point is the one your opponent saw coming until it changed direction.
